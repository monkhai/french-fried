A little help
getting moving.

French Fried is a running app for iPhone with a nine-week walk–run program, free runs, and optional running groups. The App Store release is being prepared.

Talk to a human

Email yohaiwiener@gmail.com for help, bug reports, accessibility feedback, or privacy requests. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, private keys, or your route coordinates.

What do I need?

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later, an Apple Account for Sign in with Apple, and an internet connection for account access and saving runs. The App Store version will connect to our hosted service. You won't need Xcode, a developer account, or database credentials.

Run with a friend

Each person signs in with their own Apple Account. One person creates a crew and shares its invite code. The other pastes that code in the Crew screen to join. Keep invite codes within your intended group.

Location and run guidance

Allow location access to track distance and your route. You can manage it in iPhone Settings. Voice guidance includes strong language. Voice guidance and haptics are optional and start off; enable them in the app's run guidance settings. Live Activities show your session on the Lock Screen.

Delete your account

In French Fried, open Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. This permanently removes your profile, saved runs, routes, progress, and group membership. Then follow the app's instructions to disconnect French Fried under your Apple Account's Sign in with Apple settings. If you can't access your account, email us for help; we may need to verify ownership.

Take it at your pace

The program builds toward 30 minutes of continuous running, not a guaranteed five-kilometre distance. Take rest days and repeat sessions when helpful. Stop if you feel unwell and seek appropriate professional advice.
