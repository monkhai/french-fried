Privacy, plainly.

Effective November 9, 2026. French Fried is operated by Yohai Wiener. For privacy questions or requests, email yohaiwiener@gmail.com.

What the app collects

  • Account information: your Apple sign-in identifier, email address (which may be an Apple private relay address), and the display name you choose. Apple may provide your name when you first authorize sign-in.
  • Running activity: start and end times, duration, distance, training session, completion status, program progress, and milestones.
  • Precise location: route coordinates recorded during a run when you grant location access. These are saved with your run and are private to your account.
  • Group information: your group membership, group name, and invite codes.
  • Support messages: information you choose to email us.

How it's used

We use this information to authenticate you, save and show your runs, track program progress, support your running group, respond to support requests, and maintain the service. Your device stores sign-in state, preferences, and an unfinished-run checkpoint so a run can be recovered after an interruption.

What your crew sees

Current members of your group can see your display name, progress, milestones, and run summaries, including time and distance. They cannot view your route coordinates. Members can share the group's invite code; anyone with a valid code and an account can join. Leaving a group removes your activity from its shared feed.

Service providers

Apple handles Sign in with Apple. Supabase hosts authentication and the app database. Vercel hosts this website. Google processes support email sent to our Gmail address. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and service logs to operate and secure their services. Information may be processed outside your country.

No advertising or tracking SDKs

The app does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell your personal data.

Your choices and deletion

You can update your display name, leave your crew, disable location access, and turn off optional voice or haptic guidance. Delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account. Account deletion removes your live profile, runs, routes, milestones, and group membership and signs out your sessions. An empty group and its invite are also removed. The app then shows how to disconnect Sign in with Apple in your Apple Account settings.

We retain account data while your account remains active. Provider backups and operational logs may retain copies until their scheduled expiry. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle requests and related obligations. Email us to request access, correction, or deletion, or if you cannot use the in-app deletion flow. We may verify account ownership first.

Security

The app communicates with the backend over HTTPS. Database rules restrict personal records to their owner, with specific group-summary sharing. Administrative database credentials and Apple signing keys are not included in the app or its public source repository.

Children and changes

French Fried is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us. We may update this notice as the service changes; the effective date above identifies the current version.
