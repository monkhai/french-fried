## Context for the app

Present rest days between sessions, comfortable pacing, and permission to repeat sessions. The plan targets 30 minutes of continuous running; it does not promise a five-kilometre distance. Preserve a route to general exercise preparation information and advise users concerned about their health to consult a clinician. These are general-purpose training intervals, not individual medical advice.
